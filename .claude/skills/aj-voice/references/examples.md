## The Reddit thread post

Trigger: someone sent him a Reddit thread where he was getting dragged. Posted
with a screenshot of the Apple Music lyrics for Kendrick Lamar's "wacced out
murals" attached.

> Another day. Another Reddit thread where i'm getting killed 🤣🤣🤣
>
> Someone sent me the thread this morning.
>
> "wacced out murals" is the perfect song. The love and hate is definite without a cure.
>
> They crucified Jesus. They say Lebron isn't the goat.

**Why it works, line by line:**

**"Another day. Another Reddit thread where i'm getting killed 🤣🤣🤣"** — Cold
open, mid-story. "Another" does enormous work: this is routine, not an event.
The emoji cluster lands the unbothered read before anyone can ask if it hurt.
Lowercase `i'm` left as typed.

**"Someone sent me the thread this morning."** — Deliberately flat. No drama, no
detail about who or what was said. It also quietly establishes he wasn't looking
for it.

**""wacced out murals" is the perfect song. The love and hate is definite
without a cure."** — The reference carries the whole argument. The lyric says
"being loved and hated at this level is permanent and normal," so he never has
to claim it about himself. Note `wacced` spelled the song's way, and note that
the attached lyrics screenshot is never described in the copy.

**"They crucified Jesus. They say Lebron isn't the goat."** — The deadpan
escalation pair. Two flat declaratives, absurd distance between them, zero
connective tissue. The reader assembles the point. Then the post stops — no
takeaway, no question, no hashtags.

**What is conspicuously absent:** the subreddit name, a link, a screenshot of
the comments, any rebuttal, any credential, any lesson learned, any CTA. Every
one of those would have made it a worse post, and every one of them is
something a model adds by default.

## Adding to this file

When you log a new post, keep the same three parts: what triggered it, the post
verbatim (typos intact), and a short note on what it's doing. Verbatim matters
more than the analysis — the analysis is a guess, the text is evidence.
